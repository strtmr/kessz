# KESSZ

KESSZ is the online showcase of one maker's handmade jewellery. Visitors browse the pieces and send an enquiry about the ones they want; there is no shop.

## Language

### People

**Maker**:
The person who designs and makes the jewellery and keeps the site's content up to date.
_Avoid_: owner, admin, user

**Visitor**:
Anyone browsing the public site.
_Avoid_: customer, user

### Catalogue

**Piece**:
One unique handmade item of jewellery, identified by its serial number.
_Avoid_: Artikel, article, item, product, image

**Serial number**:
The maker's own identifier for a piece, shown to visitors and quoted in enquiries.
_Avoid_: id, article number, SKU

**Category**:
The broad kind of jewellery, e.g. necklaces. A category is made up of subcategories.
_Avoid_: Hauptkategorie, main category

**Subcategory**:
A finer kind of jewellery within one category, e.g. pearl necklaces, with its own intro text. Every piece belongs to exactly one subcategory.
_Avoid_: Unterkategorie, collection, series

**Status**:
Whether a piece can still be bought: available, reserved or sold. A sold piece stays on show.
_Avoid_: visibility, stock

**Price on request**:
A piece shown without a price ("Preis auf Anfrage"). The visitor learns the price by sending an enquiry.

### Contact

**Enquiry**:
A visitor's message to the maker, either about one specific piece or general.
_Avoid_: order, request, contact mail

**Custom order**:
A piece the maker makes to a visitor's wishes ("Schmuck nach Wunsch"). It often starts from an enquiry about a sold piece.
_Avoid_: commission, Auftrag
